not cover public repository content. The Product's restriction does not
cancel that separate grant. This is permission, not evidence that this
particular package has been used for training. Public GitHub is the preferred
publication route.

Your notes and original content stay yours. You can export them and stop

continues while you comply with its terms; the free license does not promise
updates, hosting or support.

nothing to RS42. An assistant cannot agree for a person.
